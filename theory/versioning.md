# Theory Versioning

Use date-stamped immutable snapshots for major theory revisions:

```text
YYYY-MM-DD-short-description.md
```

Recommended semantic labels for derived milestones:

- HCC-0.x: exploratory formulation,
- HCC-1.0: first frozen falsifiable core,
- HCC-1.x: compatible clarifications and predictions,
- HCC-2.0+: major structural revision after evidence requires it.

Version numbers describe the hypothesis specification, not truth or maturity.

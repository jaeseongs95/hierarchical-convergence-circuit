# Current Evidence Boundary — 2026-09-16

## Directly reported in the current LLM research record

- success can depend strongly on representation and execution structure even when the target logic is held constant,
- path selection and graph generation can fail separately,
- increasing nominal D did not produce a simple monotonic failure pattern,
- identity generation and later identity rebinding are distinct failure points,
- semantic identity improved continuous graph-generation behavior under the tested conditions,
- parent binding was an important terminal failure point.

## Not established by those experiments

- biological neural circuits use the proposed primitive,
- perception and reasoning use an identical neural mechanism,
- graphs literally compile into physical circuits in the brain,
- intuition is proven to be compiled-circuit execution,
- illusions and bad intuitions are proven to be the same neural failure mechanism,
- neuromorphic hardware based on this architecture is more efficient than current architectures.

These remain hypotheses or engineering targets until directly tested.
